# Drum source-pickup regression

Use only the persistent Gregstead-Regression world and the normal regression launcher. Never copy/reset the world. Close the game before rebuilding mods.

Wait for the world and player to load. Submit `drums` through probe-command.txt and inspect drum-results.txt for CORE_TESTS_PASSED. The probe uses the real server player game-mode item-use and block-use paths. It checks water capacity, lava, material filters, mod oil/gas, flowing water, permissions, both hands, stacking, overflow and placement.

The persistent fixture is at (120,160,8), in an already-generated unclaimed chunk. Older OPAC claim-budget fixtures intentionally protect nearby chunks; leave those claims intact. Test inventory is stashed in persistent chests at (119,160,11) and (121,160,11). Do not delete their contents. Only cobblestone generated by the overflow test is discarded.

After a passing run, stop/save the client normally, restart the same world and submit `drums-persist`. Inspect drum-persistence-results.txt. It verifies 1000 mB survives restart, breaking/collection preserves the fluid, Fire Resistance is honored and creative block-use consumes one wooden barrel. No production-world tests.

The launcher must retain the pinned shader and supported development GPU-validation flag from BUILDING.md. Logs, screenshots, candidate hashes and results remain in .test-client, outside published artifacts.
